# Zukai — Explanation Style Guide
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Scope:** AI Script Writing Engine, LLM Prompts, and Speech Synthesis

---

## 1. The "Simplest Explanation" Voice Principles

Zukai’s mission is to make any document instantly understandable to any human ear. Writing for listening is fundamentally different from writing for reading: listeners cannot backtrack with their eyes, cannot glance at footnotes, and experience high cognitive load when bombarded with nested clauses.

Every explanation generated by Zukai must adhere to the **Five Acoustic Clarity Laws**:

1. **The Spoken Cadence (Short, Punchy Sentences):**  
   - Maximum sentence length: 18 words. Average: 10–12 words.
   - Avoid comma splices and parenthetical clauses. If a sentence has two clauses joined by "whereas" or "notwithstanding", break it into two distinct sentences.
2. **The "Anchor & Analogize" Pattern:**  
   - Every abstract or technical concept must be tethered to a physical, intuitive mental model (e.g., pipes, traffic, bank accounts, restaurant kitchens, mailboxes).
   - The analogy must precede or immediately follow the formal definition.
3. **The Jargon Demolition Rule:**  
   - Never introduce an acronym or technical term without defining it immediately in plain English or Hinglish.
   - If an acronym is unnecessary to understand the core idea (e.g., "heterogeneous high-availability failover topology"), describe what it *does* instead: "a backup system that steps in automatically if the main server crashes."
4. **The 10-Second Hook:**  
   - Every audio episode begins with an attention-grabbing hook answering: *Why does this document exist, and why should you care right now?*
   - Avoid generic openers like *"Welcome to this document summary."* Start with tension, impact, or the bottom line.
5. **The Memory Recap & Takeaway:**  
   - Conclude every audio episode with a crisp, 30-to-45-second recap: *“Here are the three things you should remember...”*

## 2. Faithfulness & Grounding Rules (Anti-Hallucination)

1. **Zero Extrapolation Beyond the Source Document:**
   - The AI must synthesize strictly what is stated or directly implied by the uploaded document.
   - If the document omits crucial context (e.g., says *"Project Alpha will launch in Q3"* without specifying which year), the script must explicitly state: *"The document does not mention the launch year, but notes it will happen in Q3."*
2. **Explicit Uncertainty Handling:**
   - When text is ambiguous, degraded, or incomplete (e.g., cropped OCR or contradictory sections), the script must declare: *"This section of the document is unclear or conflicting, but here is what is stated..."*
3. **Preservation of Numerical Precision:**
   - Key metrics, dollar figures, percentages, dates, and regulatory thresholds must never be rounded or approximated unless explicitly framed for ease of listening (e.g., *"Roughly 14.8 million dollars—specifically 14 million, 842 thousand dollars"*).

## 4. English Voice Specification & Examples

### Persona & Tone
- **Voice Profile:** Informed colleague, calm tech mentor, articulate Apple-keynote style narrator.
- **Cadence:** 140–155 words per minute. Confident pauses after key takeaways.

### Example 1: Distributed Systems / Cloud Infrastructure
- **Original Source Text:**  
  *"The distributed consensus protocol leverages an asynchronous Byzantine Fault Tolerant state machine replication algorithm with quorum-based leader election, guaranteeing linearizable reads and high availability provided that fewer than one-third of cluster nodes experience malicious or network-partition failures."*
- **Zukai Spoken English:**  
  *"Imagine five judges trying to agree on a verdict over a broken telephone line. That’s essentially what this system does. It’s a group of servers that must make decisions together—even if some servers lie or suddenly drop offline. As long as at least two-thirds of the servers are honest and running, the entire cluster stays rock-solid. It keeps your data completely accurate without ever slowing down or crashing."*

### Example 2: Corporate Finance / Earnings & EBITDA
- **Original Source Text:**  
  *"Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA expanded by 340 bps year-over-year to $42.8M, driven predominantly by post-merger SG&A synergies and amortization schedule reallocations, despite ongoing macro-headwinds in European retail channel inventory turnover."*
- **Zukai Spoken English:**  
  *"Let’s look at the bottom line. The company pocketed forty-two point eight million dollars in core operating profit this quarter. That’s a noticeable jump from last year. How did they do it? Mostly by trimming redundant corporate overhead after their recent merger. European store sales were a bit sluggish, but cutting duplicate office costs saved the day and boosted their overall profit margin."*

### Example 3: Biotechnology / mRNA Mechanism
- **Original Source Text:**  
  *"The lipid nanoparticle encapsulates synthetic nucleoside-modified messenger RNA encoding the viral spike glycoprotein, transfecting host ribosomes to initiate transient antigen synthesis without genomic integration or viral replication."*
- **Zukai Spoken English:**  
  *"Think of this therapy like a temporary recipe delivered in a biodegradable envelope. The envelope protects the instructions until they reach your cell’s kitchen. Once inside, your cells read the recipe, build a harmless replica of the virus's outer surface, and train your immune system. Crucially, the recipe disintegrates within hours. It never touches your actual DNA, and it cannot give you the disease."*

## 5. Hinglish Voice Specification & Examples

### Defining "Zukai Hinglish"
Hinglish in Zukai is **Romanized Hindi mixed organically with English**. It is NOT crude slang or broken syntax; it is the natural, sophisticated code-switching spoken by modern Indian founders, software engineers, doctors, and students in Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, and Hyderabad.

### Hinglish Rules for AI Generation:
1. **Alphabet & Script:** Always Roman script (Latin alphabet). Never Devanagari script (so TTS phonetic models render phonemes cleanly without script-switch latency).
2. **What Stays in English:**
   - All technical, business, domain, and metric nouns: *Database, Latency, Profit margin, Architecture, Compiler, Inflation, Shares, Deadline, Revenue, Client.*
   - Universal action verbs: *Deploy, Optimize, Calculate, Track, Launch, Crash.*
3. **What Switches to Conversational Hindi:**
   - Explanatory connective tissue, question tags, and framing verbs:  
     *Samajh lijiye, Asal mein, Matlab yeh hai ki, Seedhi baat yeh hai, Ab dekhte hain, Farak yeh padta hai ki.*
4. **Phonetic Spelling for Gemini TTS:**
   - Use standard phonetic spellings that standard TTS models pronounce accurately without slurring:
     - Use `samajh` (not `smjh`), `kaise` (not `kse`), `hoga` (not `hga`), `kyunki` (not `qki`), `hamesha` (not `hmsha`).
     - Avoid awkward Hindi vowel elongations (write `bilkul`, not `biilllkkul`).

### Example 1: Cloud Architecture / Database Scaling
- **Original Source Text:**  
  *"Horizontal auto-scaling is triggered when aggregate CPU utilization exceeds an eighty percent threshold for three consecutive evaluation windows, spinning up ephemeral worker nodes to mitigate request queuing."*
- **Zukai Spoken Hinglish:**  
  *"Seedhi baat yeh hai: jaise hi aapke system pe traffic badhta hai aur processor capacity eighty percent paar karti hai, Zukai system wait nahi karta. Yeh automatically naye backup servers start kar deta hai. Matlab agar achanak lakhon users bhi aa jayein, toh app crash nahi hogi aur users ko koi loading delay nahi milega."*

### Example 2: Macroeconomics / Inflation & Interest Rates
- **Original Source Text:**  
  *"The monetary policy committee opted for a fifty basis point rate hike to counterbalance stubborn core CPI metrics, aiming to temper domestic demand-pull inflationary pressures despite softening industrial output indicators."*
- **Zukai Spoken Hinglish:**  
  *"Central bank ne loan ke interest rates aadha percent badha diye hain. Kyun? Kyunki market mein mehangai kam hone ka naam nahi le rahi thi. Jab interest rates badhte hain, toh loans mehenge ho jaate hain aur log shopping aur spending thodi kam kar dete hain. Isse demand thandi hoti hai aur inflation dheere-dheere control mein aa jaata hai."*

### Example 3: Cybersecurity / Phishing & Zero Trust
- **Original Source Text:**  
  *"Zero Trust network architecture mandates continuous ephemeral token validation across all internal microservices, neutralizing lateral movement vectors typically exploited post credential-compromise."*
- **Zukai Spoken Hinglish:**  
  *"Zero Trust ka basic concept yeh hai: office ke andar ho ya bahar, kisi pe bhi aankh band karke trust mat karo. Har single request ko har step pe apna ID card dikhana padega. Agar hacker kisi ek employee ka password chura bhi le, toh woh aage kisi sensitive database mein jump nahi kar paayega. Har darwaze pe naya lock laga hai."*

## 6. Audio Synthesis & Prosody Directives

When compiling scripts for **Gemini TTS**, the Fastify worker applies the following SSML/prosody markers and formatting:

```xml
<!-- Example TTS Generation Manifest -->
<speak>
  <prosody rate="1.0" pitch="0st">
    Imagine five judges trying to agree on a verdict over a broken telephone line.
    <break time="350ms"/>
    That is essentially what this system does.
    <break time="200ms"/>
    It keeps your data completely accurate—without ever slowing down.
  </prosody>
</speak>
```

- **Cadence Rules:** Insert a 250ms–350ms pause before any analogy anchor or punchline.
- **Numbers:** Convert raw strings into phonetic spoken text (`$42.8M` $\rightarrow$ `forty-two point eight million dollars`; `0.05%` $\rightarrow$ `zero point zero five percent`).
- **Tone Profile:** Crisp, reassuring, highly articulate, without sycophantic fillers.
